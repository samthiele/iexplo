Operating mines continually change: pit walls and tailings move, surfaces weather, and new material is exposed or stored. Capturing those changes at site scale needs more than occasional surveys. We combine satellite Earth observation with UAV and in situ sensors so that deformation, surface composition and the shallow subsurface can be watched together.

That work is centred on **MOSMIN**, a Horizon Europe action for multiscale observation of mine operations. Copernicus supplies wide-area deformation and composition maps; drones and ground sensors, machine-learning fusion and distributed fibre-optic sensing fill the gaps at higher resolution. The same stack supports geotechnical and environmental monitoring and the valorisation of mining-related deposits.

The practical goals are lower safety and environmental risk, greener observation tools, and transparent ESG reporting — without waiting for the next field campaign.
