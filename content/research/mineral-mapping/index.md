We use various imaging sensors to acquire hyperspectral data over rock samples and drill-cores. These sensors cover the visible and near infrared (VNIR), short-wave infrared (SWIR), mid-wave infrared (MWIR) and long-wave infrared (LWIR) ranges of the electromagnetic spectrum. Various minerals show characteristic spectral features in different parts of the spectrum, so combining these datasets can provide a wealth of information on sample mineralogy.

We use both expert-rule systems based on the interpretation of spectra and machine-learning methods to map minerals rapidly. We have developed Python-based toolboxes for this purpose. By identifying and mapping minerals quickly, we can target areas with high potential for specific mineralisation.
