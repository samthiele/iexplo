Digitisation of mechanical separation (for example wind sifting of shredded Li-ion batteries). HIF records on-line particle size, shape and material properties of input and product streams with a multi-sensor setup on a conveyor, and consolidates method-specific data in real time. The approach is intended to transfer beyond Li-ion recycling.
