TheiaX GmbH is a HIF/HZDR spin-off applying hyperspectral imaging and machine learning to exploration and mining. Incubation was via Helmholtz Enterprise, with early services through HZDR Innovation GmbH; the legal entity was founded in December 2021. Work includes tripod and drone HSI, drill-core HSI, and machine-learning data fusion.
