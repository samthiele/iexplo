Autonomous, task-distributed drone network for isolated remote targets. HIF leads drone design, spectral imaging and machine learning; KIT-IIIT contributes sensor fusion, navigation, communications and automated decisions; CASUS contributes machine learning and real-time anomaly detection.

Open software: [github.com/hifexplo/autotarget](https://github.com/hifexplo/autotarget).
