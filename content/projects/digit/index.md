**DigIT** develops digital technologies for non-invasive, near-mine exploration of critical raw materials. Mining continually exposes geological information on pit walls and underground faces, but time and current methods mean most of it is lost. DigIT captures that information quickly and feeds it into deposit-scale exploration, so discoveries can sit next to active or historic mines where infrastructure already exists and social and environmental constraints are often easier to meet.

The work has four strands: safe, objective mapping of mining areas by combining digital outcrops with visible–SWIR and long-wave infrared hyperspectral imaging (including underground workflows); self-updating geological models that absorb new mine-face data; physical-chemical simulation of ore-forming processes to derive exploration targets; and data standards that keep mapping archives interoperable and reusable. HIF leads hyperspectral mapping, geometallurgical upscaling and neural-field 3-D models; Terranigma extends LiquidEarth for immersive mapping and real-time model updates.

The methods are demonstrated in the Sn–(Li)–W-rich Ore Mountains (Erzgebirge) and the Cu-rich Iberian Pyrite Belt. Project site: [digital-exploration.de](https://digital-exploration.de/).
