I am Junaidh Shaik Fareedh. I completed my bachelor's in Aeronautical Engineering in India and then moved to Sweden for a master's in Aerospace Engineering, which included building satellite structures and space-approved electronics that are now in orbit, launched by ISRO and ESA. I pursued a second master's in Robotics Engineering in Germany, working on satellite-simulating pods and space rovers for testing AI algorithms.

My passion to build and fly winged aircraft, multirotors and heavy-lifting automated drones is growing by the day.
