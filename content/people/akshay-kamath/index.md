Akshay Kamath is a Geophysicist contributing to the iExplo / HZDR-HIF team. He specialises in applying advanced geophysical methods and neural fields for subsurface characterisation, with a particular focus on potential fields and 3D geological models. His research aims to enhance our understanding of complex geological structures, supporting innovative solutions for resource exploration and subsurface understanding.
