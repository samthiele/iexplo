Akshay Kamath is a Geophysicist contributing to the iExplo / HZDR-HIF team. He specialises in applying advanced geophysical methods for subsurface characterisation, with a particular focus on seismic data processing and inversion. His research aims to enhance our understanding of complex geological structures, supporting innovative solutions for resource exploration and environmental monitoring challenges.
