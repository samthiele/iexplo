Gopi is a Robotics Research Engineer specialising in industrial robotics, mobile robotics and autonomous systems. With a Master’s degree in Robotics Engineering from France, Gopi has a comprehensive background in developing and optimising robotic systems. His expertise spans software and embedded systems development, mechanical design and the integration of robots with vision systems, sensors and industrial networks. Gopi is passionate about product development and the testing of autonomous robots and manipulators. His research interests include the design, dynamics and control of legged robots.
