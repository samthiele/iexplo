As a structural geologist, Andrés Melo Giron specialises in deciphering the intricate architecture of geological formations. His research at iExplo / HZDR-HIF applies a multi-scale approach, from regional mapping to microstructural analysis, to understand rock deformation processes and their significance for subsurface exploration and resource characterisation.
