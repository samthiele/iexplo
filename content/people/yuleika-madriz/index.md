Yuleika Madriz is a research engineer within the Division of Exploration at the Helmholtz Institute Freiberg for Resource Technology in Germany. Her research interest lies on drone-borne geophysics for non-invasive mineral exploration, with focus on magnetic data acquisition, processing and interpretation. She received her M.Sc. degree in Sustainable Mining and Remediation Management from TU Bergakademie Freiberg, Germany. She holds a B.Sc. degree in Geophysical Engineering from the Simon Bolivar University in Caracas, Venezuela.
