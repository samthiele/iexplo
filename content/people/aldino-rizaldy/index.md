I am a photogrammetry and deep learning enthusiast. I’ve done many works on 3D reconstruction from various large-format or small-format aerial cameras, carried by manned or unmanned aerial vehicles. In the last couple of years, I’ve been working on deep learning for images and point clouds. Now, I’m interested in point cloud segmentation with additional hyperspectral features.
