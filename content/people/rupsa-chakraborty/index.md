I am a Postdoctoral Researcher based at HIF, primarily affiliated with CASUS. My educational background is in Applied Geology and I am very interested in the potential of hyperspectral remote sensing in this niche. I acquired formal training in remote sensing, specialising in geosciences, at the Indian Institute of Remote Sensing under ISRO. I pursued my doctoral degree from Massey University in New Zealand. My doctoral thesis focused on integrating biogeochemical attributes of the vegetation cover with subsurface mineralisation, employing airborne hyperspectral data for mineral exploration.
