I have a Ph.D. in geology and generally speaking I consider myself a remote sensing geologist. My research interests include the use of multi-scale remote sensing data (including drone-borne data) for mineral exploration. My skills are focused toward geological applications, such as the use of spectroscopy for mineral identification, multi- and hyperspectral image processing as well as geological field work.
