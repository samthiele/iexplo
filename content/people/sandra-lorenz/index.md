My research interests are in drone-based, terrestrial and lab-scale (hyper) spectral imaging for non-invasive mineral exploration, or more specifically:

- innovative exploration technologies and optical remote sensing
- digital processing of multi-modal image and point-cloud data
- spectroscopy, spectral imaging and material mapping
- sensor development and data acquisition
- autonomous airborne and terrestrial platforms
- machine learning and data fusion
