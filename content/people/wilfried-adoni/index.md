Wilfried Yves Hamilton Adoni is a Scientific Researcher at HZDR-HIF and CASUS working on data integration and machine learning for autonomous multi-UAV systems. He joined the elite group of young Big Data Specialists with IBM BigInsights V2.1 in 2015. He received the B.S. degree in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics from Hassan II University of Casablanca, Morocco in 2012, the M.E. degree in Operational Research and System Optimisation from the same university in 2014, and his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics from the Science Faculty, Hassan II University of Casablanca.

He served as Assistant/Associate Professor of Computer Science at the International University of Casablanca and Temporary Assistant, Teaching and Research (ATER) at the French CentraleSupélec School of Casablanca, and Lecturer of Computer Science at Hassan II University of Casablanca. He has been a scientific supervisor at the African Institute of Mathematical Sciences (Senegal and Rwanda) and lecturer in several universities in Africa. He is Vice-President of the Ivorian Society of Operational Research and a reviewer for journals related to big data, AI and HPC.

His research interests include autonomous drones/UAVs, big data, graph databases, machine learning, path-finding algorithms and traffic-flow analysis.
