By Samiran Das.

A hyperspectral cube is naturally an order-3 tensor. Tucker, PARAFAC/CP, tensor-ring, tensor-train and t-SVD decompositions support dimensionality reduction, feature extraction, denoising and unmixing. Useful libraries include n-way, TensorLab, the MATLAB tensor toolbox and TensorLy.
