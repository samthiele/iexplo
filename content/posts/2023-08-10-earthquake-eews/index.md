Take a look at this paper on **IoT-Cloud-Based Earthquake Early Warning Systems where Willfried Yves Hamilton Adoni is co-author.

The work has been summarised in the online article at encylclopedia.pub:
[https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/invitation/102851](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/invitation/102851)

"There are strong correlations between earthquakes, climate changes, and mining activities. Many scientists have predicted that the frequency of earthquakes will keep increasing. In the first paper on the idea of earthquake early warning systems (EEWS) in 1985 was presented. These systems are networks of ground-based sensors that alert users when the earth starts to tremble."

The full article titled: *Early Detection of Earthquakes Using IoT and Cloud Infrastructure: A Survey. *Can be found here:

[https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/15/15/11713](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/15/15/11713)
