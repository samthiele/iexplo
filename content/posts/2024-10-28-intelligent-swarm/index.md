A team of researchers from Helmholtz-Zentrum Dresden-Rossendorf has introduced a concept for autonomous aerial systems in the paper *Intelligent Swarm: Concept, Design and Validation of Self-Organized UAVs Based on Leader–Followers Paradigm for Autonomous Mission Planning*.

The study proposes a framework for organising UAV swarms through a central leader drone that guides follower drones. The approach is intended to improve coordination, situational adaptability, and robustness of the group under changing mission requirements.

The work was a collaboration between HZDR-CASUS and HZDR-HIF, with contributions from Richard Gloaguen, Sandra Lorenz, Thomas D. Kühne, Wilfried Hamilton, Junaidh Shaik Fareedh, Aastha Singh and Yuleika Madriz Diaz.

Full paper: [Drones 8(10):575](https://www.mdpi.com/2504-446X/8/10/575)
