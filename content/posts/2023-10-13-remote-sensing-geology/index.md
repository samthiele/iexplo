A while ago, colleagues and I wrote a chapter in the *Encyclopedia of Geology* (2nd edition) on geological remote sensing.

We tried to give a brief but concise overview of the sensors used, the different platforms, the processing of the data and typical geological applications, plus a list of papers for further reading.

Booysen, R., Gloaguen, R., Lorenz, S., Zimmermann, R., & Nex, P. A. M., 2021. Geological Remote Sensing. In: Alderton, D. & Elias, S. A. (eds.) *Encyclopedia of Geology*, 2nd edition, vol. 6, pp. 301–314. Academic Press.
