Excellent new contribution led by Ahmed J. Afifi in collaboration with Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) IIIT.

We want to boost 3D Earth-observation and image processing. The Earth is not flat — so let's deal with real data. It is time to move from 2D to 3D datasets.

Here we provide a new hyperspectral benchmark dataset with VNIR, SWIR and LWIR attributes of very unstructured surfaces. Open-access paper, open-source codes and an open dataset.
