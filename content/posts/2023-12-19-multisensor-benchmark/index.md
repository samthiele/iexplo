We want to boost 3D EOand Image processing. The Earth is not flat... So let's deal with real data.

It's time to move one from 2D to 3D datasets.

Here we provide a new hyperspectral benchmark dataset with VNIR, SWIR and LWIR attributes of very unstructured surfaces. Who wants to beat current point nets? Open access paper, open source codes and open source dataset.
