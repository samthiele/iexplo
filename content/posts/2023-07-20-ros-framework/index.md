ROS is middleware, not an operating system. ROS 2 replaces the ROS 1 transport with DDS and runs on Ubuntu, macOS and Windows. Noetic’s end of life was May 2025; new swarm and robot work should start on ROS 2. A short case study covers an orchestrator with MTConnect.
