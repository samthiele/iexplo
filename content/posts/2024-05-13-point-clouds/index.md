A short FAQ on 3D point clouds: they store XYZ coordinates and often RGB (sometimes called “4D”). Advantages include precision, speed of acquisition, and straightforward visualisation.

LiDAR measures geometry; photogrammetric point clouds usually add colour. Classification assigns semantic labels to regions, while segmentation isolates individual objects — the city versus the buildings. Applications include mapping, disaster risk, urban mobility, forest management, intelligent transport and DEM generation.
