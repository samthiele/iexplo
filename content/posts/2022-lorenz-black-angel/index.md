Hyperspectral imaging is an innovative technology for non-invasive mapping, with increasing applications in many sectors. This data paper releases three-dimensional, kilometre-scale hyperspectral observations of well-exposed Zn–Pb mineralisation at Black Angel Mountain, Greenland, plus a tutorial.

The figures show (a) a true-colour RGB photogrammetric point cloud, (b) a false-colour iron index (red = Fe³⁺, green = Fe²⁺, blue = FeOH), and (c) a minimum-wavelength map for carbonate.
