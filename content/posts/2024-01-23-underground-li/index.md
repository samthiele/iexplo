In this study we propose a workflow for using hyperspectral imaging for geological mapping in underground mining. We evaluated sensors and lighting set-ups in the laboratory and acquired data in a German underground mine. The corrected data were used to map mineral abundance and estimate Li content, validated with laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy.

Despite the challenges of underground illumination and geometry, the work aims to establish hyperspectral sensors in the extractive industry — to increase raw-material supply, advance digitalisation, and reduce environmental impact and mining risk.

Paper: [The Photogrammetric Record](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/phor.12457)
