Excited to share a new paper on spectral characterisation of battery components from Li-ion battery recycling processes, led by Julia Richter. The work was a collaboration between TU Bergakademie Freiberg and the Helmholtz Institute Freiberg within the BMBF-funded project DIGISORT.

Addressing the need for efficient recycling strategies amid rising demand for Li-ion batteries, the study uses optical sensors for non-contact, real-time monitoring of battery-component detection. Investigating millimetre-scale particles from end-of-life batteries with five reflectance sensors, we identified the visible to near-infrared range (400–1000 nm) as optimal for discriminating aluminium, copper and other battery components, and we provide practical recommendations for industry.

Full article: [Metals 14(2):147](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4701/14/2/147)
