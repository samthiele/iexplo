Mineral exploration in the West Greenland flood-basalt province is attractive because of its resemblance to magmatic sulphide-rich systems. At Qullissat on northern Disko we combined UAS magnetics and multispectral data to build a 3-D magnetisation-vector model that includes remanence acquired during a reversal, and to account for landslide-rotated blocks when targeting Ni–Cu–Co–PGE–Au.
